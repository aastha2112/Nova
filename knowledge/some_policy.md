# Nova Website Policies

## Refunds
Subscriptions and digital data passes can be refunded within 7 days of purchase if unused. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days. Custom data reports cannot be refunded.

## Delivery & Access
Digital space data and satellite logs are delivered instantly to your account. Physical prints or planetarium media ship in 3 to 5 working days. Delivery is free on orders above ₹499. For orders below ₹499, a fee of ₹40 applies. We currently deliver physical items only within India.

## Payments
We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards, and cash on delivery for physical items up to ₹3000.

Our support team is available from 9 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday.
